**(Full Name: ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAbd Allāh)**: عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللهِ

Al-Suhaylī (d. 581 AH / 1185 AD) was a brilliant blind scholar from **Al-Andalus** (Islamic Spain). He was a master of Arabic grammar, Hadith, and history.

He is most famous for his masterpiece, **"Al-Rawḍ al-Unuf"** (الرَّوْضُ الْأُنُفُ), which is an extensive commentary on the _Sīrah_ (Prophetic Biography) of **Ibn Hishām**. In this work, he solved many of the difficult genealogical puzzles and linguistic mysteries found in the lineages.. He is often praised for his unique ability to explain _why_ certain tribes were named the way they were.
