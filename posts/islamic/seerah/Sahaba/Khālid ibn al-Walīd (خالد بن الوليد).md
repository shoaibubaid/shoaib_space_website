# Khālid ibn al-Walīd (خالد بن الوليد, d. 21 AH / 642 CE)

- Lineage & Clan: Banū Makhzūm, Quraysh.

- Conversion: Embraced Islam in 7–8 AH, shortly before the Conquest of Mecca.

- Key Role: Given the title Sayf Allāh al-Maslūl ("The Drawn Sword of God") by the Prophet Muhammad. He was the supreme tactical general of early Islam, undefeated in over 50 military engagements.

- Legacy: Commanded the Ridda Wars under Abū Bakr, led the lightning march across the desert to open the conquest of Sasanian Iraq, and engineered the decisive Byzantine defeat at the Battle of Yarmūk.
