# ‘Alī ibn Abī Ṭālib (علي بن أبي طالب, d. 40 AH / 661 CE)

- Lineage & Clan: Banū Hāshim, Quraysh; first cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet (married to Fāṭimah).

- Conversion: The first male child/youth to accept Islam.

- Key Role: Celebrated for unparalleled chivalry, combat valor (famous for the duals at Badr, Uḥud, and Khaybar), judicial acumen, and spiritual eloquence.

- Legacy: Reigned as the fourth Rightly-Guided Caliph (al-Khulafā’ al-Rāshidūn) from 35 to 40 AH, a turbulent period marked by the first civil war (Fitnah), before his assassination in Kūfah by a Kharijite.
