# Mu‘ādh ibn Jabal (معاذ بن جبل, d. 18 AH / 639 CE)
- Lineage & Tribe: Banū Khazraj, the Anṣār of Medina.
- Conversion: Accepted Islam at around age 18 during the Second Pledge of al-‘Aqabah.
- Key Role: The foremost jurist and legal scholar of his generation among the Companions; the Prophet described him as the most knowledgeable in matters of Ḥalāl (lawful) and Ḥarām (unlawful).
- Legacy: Dispatched by the Prophet to Yemen as chief justice, administrator, and teacher (where the famous Hadith on using Ijtihād / legal reasoning was articulated). He later joined the campaign in Syria, where he succumbed to the Plague of ‘Amwās in 18 AH at the young age of roughly 33–38.
