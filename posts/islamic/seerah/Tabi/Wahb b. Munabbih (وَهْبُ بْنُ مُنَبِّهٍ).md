[[Tabi]]

Timeline: Born ~34 AH (654 CE) – Died ~110 or 114 AH (728–732 CE) 
Brief intro: Born in Yemen to a family of Persian origin, he is recognized as one of the most prominent early Islamic historians and a pioneer in early biographical writing (_Maghazi_). He is particularly famous for being a major transmitter of _Isra'iliyyat_ (Judeo-Christian traditions and lore) into early Islamic literature, drawing heavily on his extensive knowledge of earlier scriptures. He also served as a judge (qadi) in Sana'a.
