[[Tabi]]

**Timeline:** Born ~60 AH (680 CE) – Died 117 AH (735 CE).
**Brief intro:** Based in Basra, Qatada was a blind scholar of extraordinary memory, often cited as one of the most brilliant minds of his generation. He was a master of Quranic exegesis (_Tafsir_), Hadith, Arabic linguistics, and genealogy. He is particularly famous for his extensive contributions to the field of Tafsir, where his explanations are frequently quoted alongside those of al-Suddi and Mujahid in classical commentaries.
