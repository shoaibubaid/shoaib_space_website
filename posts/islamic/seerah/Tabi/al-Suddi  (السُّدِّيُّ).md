al-Suddi / Isma'il b. 'Abd al-Rahman (السُّدِّيُّ / إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ السُّدِّيُّ)
[[Tabi]]

Timeline: Died 127 AH (745 CE) 
Brief intro: Commonly known as "al-Suddi al-Kabir" (the Elder) to distinguish him from a later, lesser-known narrator, he was originally from the Hijaz but settled and taught in Kufa. He is best known for his foundational work in Quranic exegesis (_Tafsir_) and history. His Tafsir is widely cited in later works (like those of al-Tabari) and relies heavily on chains of transmission going back to prominent Companions of the Prophet, such as Ibn 'Abbas and Ibn Mas'ud.
