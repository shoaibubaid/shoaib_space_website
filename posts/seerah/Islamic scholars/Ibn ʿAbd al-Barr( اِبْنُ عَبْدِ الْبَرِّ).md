**Abū ʿUmar Yūsuf ibn ʿAbd Allāh:** أَبُو عُمَرَ يُوسُفُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللهِ

He was the preeminent 11th-century scholar of Muslim Spain (**al-Andalus**). Like Ibn Mākūlā, he was a master of **genealogy** and **Hadith**. He is particularly famous for his book _Al-Inbāh_ (الاِنْبَاهُ), which focuses specifically on the lineages of the Arab tribes you've been asking about.
