 Later Scholar (Student of [[Al-Bukhārī (الْبُخَارِيُّ)]]).
 
 His full name is **Abū ʿĪsā Muḥammad**. He was a master of Hadith from Central Asia. He is most famous for his _Sunan_, where he not only collected Hadiths but also categorized them by their legal strength (Sahih, Hasan, Da'if) and explained the opinions of the jurists—making his work a vital "encyclopedia" of scholarly debate.
