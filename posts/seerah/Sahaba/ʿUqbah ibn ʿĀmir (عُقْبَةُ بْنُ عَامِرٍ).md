He is a [[Sahaba]]. He was a distinguished **Sahabi** (Companion) of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. He was a member of the **Juhaynah** (جُهَيْنَةُ) tribe, which is a branch of the **Quḍāʿah** (قُضَاعَةُ) lineage.

ʿUqbah was famous for several things:

- **The Archer:** He was an expert marksman and a great lover of horses.
- **The Scholar-General:** He served as the governor of **Egypt** and was one of the few companions who was also an expert in the Quran, inheritance laws, and poetry.
- **The Mus-haf:** He had his own personal manuscript (Mus-haf) of the Quran.

Since he was from **Juhaynah**, he is a key figure for anyone studying the history of the **Quḍāʿah** tribes.
